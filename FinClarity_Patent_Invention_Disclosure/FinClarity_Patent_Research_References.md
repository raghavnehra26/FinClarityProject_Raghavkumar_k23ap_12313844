# FinClarity patent research references

Research date: 2026-11-19. Patent records are technical comparison material, not a legal opinion. Bibliographic title and abstract descriptions were checked against the linked patent publication records. Legal status, claim scope, family relationships, and freedom to operate require professional review.

### US 2022/0004923 A1 — Systems and methods for model explanation

- Abstract-level summary: Describes generating explanation information for machine-learning scores associated with credit applicants, including feature-level explanation measures.
- Relevance and distinction: The disclosed examples concern credit-applicant explanation processes and comparative reference applicants. FinClarity's saved classifier instead labels membership in two unmatched historical source files and does not make credit decisions.
- Source: https://patents.google.com/patent/US20220004923A1/en
### CA 3,110,889 A1 — Conversational business tool

- Abstract-level summary: Describes a conversational tool that receives a query, accesses business data through interfaces, and returns an analysis in conversational form.
- Relevance and distinction: The disclosure addresses business data access and conversational analytics generally; it does not describe FinClarity's specific historical-file classifier, SHAP evidence, and project-aware local assistant configuration.
- Source: https://patents.google.com/patent/CA3110889A1/en
### US 2025/0191062 A1 — Auto-adjudication process via machine learning

- Abstract-level summary: Describes receiving loan application attributes, using an ML model to identify adjudication rules, and determining and communicating a loan approval outcome.
- Relevance and distinction: That application is directed to loan adjudication and approval decisions. FinClarity explicitly does not perform either function and instead classifies source-file membership in selected historical datasets.
- Source: https://patents.google.com/patent/US20250191062A1/en
